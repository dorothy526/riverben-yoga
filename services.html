Our Class Offerings

All classes are designed to be accessible and supportive. No previous experience is required.

Class Types

Gentle Yoga

Slow, mindful movement with emphasis on breath and comfort. Ideal for beginners and those seeking a softer practice.

Vinyasa Flow

A moderately paced practice that links breath with movement. Modifications are always offered.

Restorative Yoga

Deeply relaxing poses supported by props. Perfect for recovery, stress relief, and older adults.

Private Sessions

One-on-one sessions tailored to your needs, goals, and physical considerations. Available by appointment.
