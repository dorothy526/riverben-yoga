About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Founded to create a welcoming space for every body and every experience level, Riverbend Yoga Studio focuses on accessibility, kindness, and community.

Meet Our Instructors

Our teachers are experienced, patient, and committed to inclusive teaching. Each instructor brings a unique background while sharing the same core values of support and safety.

Accessibility Accommodations

  • Chairs and props available in every class
  • Clear verbal cues and demonstration options
  • Ground-floor studio with accessible entrance
  • Options for seated or standing practice

Studio Policies

We ask students to arrive a few minutes early, wear comfortable clothing, and practice with kindness toward themselves and others. Please inform your instructor of any injuries or conditions so we can support you appropriately.
